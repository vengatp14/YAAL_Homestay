About Doordarshan

India's Premier Public Service Broadcaster

Doordarshan, a Public Service Broadcaster of India, functioning under the aegis of Prasar Bharati, is one of the largest Broadcasting Organizations in the world in-terms of coverage and infrastructure like studios and transmitters.

Television Centre, Chennai was inaugurated on 15th August 1975 and it served a radius of 20 km. Subsequently, a permanent High Power Transmitter was commissioned on July 1976 in order to serve 80 km radius covering 12,000 square km.

Satellite link was established in the year 1982 to facilitate to down link National Programmes transmitted from Delhi Kendra along with color transmission. Thereafter, Doordarshan had seen a lot of expansion in the form of transmitters and consequently its reach drastically.

A High power Transmitter with 10 KW capacity was commissioned on 14th Jan 1987 at Kodaikanal Hill top to relay the Programmes to reach the far-flung areas.

On the 15th September 1997, the prestigious Prasar Bharathi declared autonomous status for 'All India Radio' and 'Doordarshan' to produce and broadcast their choice of programmes.

Regional Language 'Tamil Satellite Channel viz., Podhigai started its functioning on the Pongal day i.e., 15.1.2001 with round the clock transmission. Considering the genre of programmes telecast it is coined as 'infotainment Channel'.

New format of accessing satellite to receive preferred channels viz., 'Direct to Home' was commissioned by the Honourable Prime Minister, on 16th December 2004 with DD Direct Plus on Doordarshan's DTH platform.

At present, the Regional service – Terrestrial Transmission of Podhigai channel is being relayed by 7 High Power Transmitters, 12 Low Power Transmitters to serve entire Tamilnadu state.
